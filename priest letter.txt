================================================================
REQUEST FOR PASTORAL REVIEW
Bilingual Confession Preparation Website
================================================================


Dear Father,

I have built a small bilingual (English + Malayalam) website to 
help the faithful prepare for the Sacrament of Reconciliation. 
It walks a person through the Ten Commandments, the Seven 
Capital Sins, and a short reflection on bad confession itself. 
Everything runs privately in the user's browser — no data is 
sent to any server.

The site is live at:

    https://confession-app-production.up.railway.app


Before I share it more widely, I would be grateful if you could 
review the CONTENT — not the code. Specifically, four things:


----------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE EXAMINATION-OF-CONSCIENCE LIST ITSELF
----------------------------------------------------------------

The full list is in the app. Tap "Begin" on the welcome page, 
then step through the sections. It is also available in a single 
file (data/sins.json) in the source code.

I have tried to be faithful to traditional Catholic teaching, 
but I expect there are items that are too broad, mis-categorized, 
redundant, or missing.

What I would value: any items you would remove, any you would 
rephrase, any categories I have overlooked.


----------------------------------------------------------------
2. THREE CATEGORIES I HAVE TREATED WITH SPECIAL CARE
----------------------------------------------------------------

These three gave me the most pause:

(a) SUICIDAL THOUGHTS AND ATTEMPTED SUICIDE

I originally had these as items a user could tick. I have since 
removed them from the sin list entirely, because a checkbox is 
not the right medium for someone in crisis.

They are now covered by a safeguarding panel on the Welcome 
page with crisis line numbers (iCall, AASRA).

What I would value: confirmation that this is the right pastoral 
approach, and whether the crisis line numbers and wording of the 
panel are appropriate.

(b) SEXUAL ABUSE, CHILD ABUSE, RAPE

The risk is that a victim of these sins may tick them as if they 
were their own. I have reworded the two abuse entries as 
perpetrator-direction:

    "Have I sexually harassed or pressured another person?"
    "Have I abused a child in any way?"

...each with a note clarifying that victims are not culpable. 
The "Rape" entry has been removed entirely.

What I would value: your judgment on whether this framing is 
right, and whether any victim of abuse might still accidentally 
confess something done TO them.

(c) PRIDE, ANXIETY, DOUBTS ABOUT FAITH, "ATHEISTIC THOUGHTS"

These are easy to misunderstand. I have attached short 
explanatory notes to each, making clear that a passing thought 
or feeling is not a sin — only deliberate consent is.

What I would value: confirmation that the notes are 
theologically precise, and whether any other items in the list 
deserve a similar clarification.


----------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE PASTORAL GUARDRAILS
----------------------------------------------------------------

The app displays short messages at four moments — the welcome 
page, each section, the summary page, and inside the generated 
PDF — reminding the user that a contrite heart matters more 
than a complete list.

Sample text:

    "You do not need to remember every sin. The goal is to help 
     you prepare your heart, not to produce a perfect list. If 
     you forget something, that is okay — God already knows, 
     and His mercy is greater than any list."

What I would value: whether this tone is appropriate. I have 
tried to keep the seriousness of sin while guarding against 
scrupulosity. If any wording feels too casual or too heavy, 
please tell me.


----------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE PRAYERS
----------------------------------------------------------------

The Act of Contrition, Confiteor, and Opening of Confession 
are shown in English and Malayalam side by side, so the user 
can see both.

What I would value: confirmation that the Malayalam is the 
correct traditional text, and that no wording has shifted 
theologically in translation.


----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT I AM NOT ASKING
----------------------------------------------------------------

I am not asking you to test the website, review the code, or 
comment on its design. Only the content and the pastoral 
framing.

Any feedback, however brief, will be received with gratitude.

If it is easier for you to review on paper, I can print and 
deliver the full list. Please let me know what form is most 
convenient.


Thank you, Father, for your time and your care for the 
faithful.
