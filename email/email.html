Request for Pastoral Review

A bilingual confession preparation website

Dear Father,

I have built a small bilingual (English + Malayalam) website to help the faithful prepare for the Sacrament of Reconciliation. It walks a person through the Ten Commandments, the Seven Capital Sins, and a short reflection on bad confession itself.

Everything runs privately in the user's browser. Nothing they type or select is sent to any server.

Open the website → confession-app-production.up.railway.app

What I am asking

Before I share it more widely, I would be grateful if you could review the content — not the code. Specifically:

  • The examination-of-conscience list — any items to remove, rephrase, or add.
  • Three sensitive categories — how I have handled suicidal thoughts, abuse, and doubts about faith.
  • The pastoral guardrails — short messages reminding the user that a contrite heart matters more than a perfect list.
  • The prayers — that the Malayalam text is the correct traditional version.

I am not asking you to test the website or review the code. Only the content and the pastoral framing.

How I have handled three sensitive areas

Suicidal thoughts. I originally had these as tickable items on the sin list. I have removed them entirely, because a checkbox is not the right medium for someone in crisis. They are now covered by a safeguarding panel with crisis line numbers.

Abuse and rape. The risk is that a victim may tick these as if they were their own. I have reworded the abuse entries as perpetrator-direction ("Have I abused a child in any way?", "Have I sexually harassed or pressured another person?"), with a note clarifying that victims are not culpable. The "Rape" entry has been removed entirely.

Pride, anxiety, doubts about faith. These are easy to misunderstand. Each carries a short note making clear that a passing thought or feeling is not a sin — only deliberate consent is.

For anyone who is suffering

If you are thinking about harming yourself, if you have been abused, or if you are carrying a wound that feels too heavy to bear alone — please do not go through it alone. Speak to a priest, a doctor, a counsellor, or someone you trust.

You are not alone, and what happened to you is not your sin.

Thank you, Father, for your time and your care for the faithful.

With respect,
Edwin
